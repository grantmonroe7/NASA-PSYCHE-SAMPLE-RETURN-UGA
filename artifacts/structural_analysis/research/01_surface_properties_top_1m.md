# Top 1 m Surface Properties of (16) Psyche by Region

## Summary Consensus (2020–2025 literature)
Psyche is **heterogeneous**. The upper ~1 m (radar-sensing depth) is a mixture of metal (Fe-Ni), low-Fe silicates (orthopyroxene), and minor exogenic carbonaceous material, with significant porosity. Bulk density of the asteroid is ~3.4–4.2 g cm⁻³; the uppermost meter shows radar albedos implying regolith bulk densities ~2.6–4.8 g cm⁻³.

### Regional Variations
- **High-radar-albedo / high-optical-albedo patches** (at least four identified): elevated metal fraction, possibly more coherent or less porous metal-rich material.
- **Lowlands of large depressions**: lower thermal inertia → finer or more porous regolith / fractured material / silicate-rich veneer overlying metal-richer substrate.
- **Global average**: mixed metal–silicate regolith, fine-grained silicate component detected in mid-IR, radar consistent with metal-bearing regolith of high porosity or metal content.
